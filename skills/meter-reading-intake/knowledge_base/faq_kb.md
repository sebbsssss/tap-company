**Q: When is rent due?**

## Utilities

**Q: Are utilities included in my rent?**
A: Utility charges (electricity, water) are billed separately based on your actual usage. A utility sample is included in your tenancy agreement to set expectations. [GENERIC-confirm — confirm if gas is included or separate]

**Q: How is my utility bill calculated?**
A: Your usage is metered at the room and/or unit level. The Finance team sends a monthly utility statement showing your meter readings and the amount due.

**Q: When is my utility bill due?**
A: Utility bills are typically due alongside your monthly rent on the 1st. [GENERIC-confirm — confirm exact billing cycle with Finance]

**Q: I think my utility bill is too high. What should I do?**
A: Please contact the Finance team with your concern and they will review your meter readings. We can escalate a dispute if needed.

## Maintenance & Repairs

**Q: How do I raise a maintenance request?**
A: You can raise a maintenance request here via WhatsApp — just describe the issue, your property, and your room number. We will log a ticket and our team will follow up.

**Q: How long will it take to fix my issue?**
A: Response times depend on priority. Emergency issues (power trip, pipe burst, gate stuck) are attended to within hours. Non-urgent issues (light bulb, furniture) are typically resolved within 1–5 business days. [GENERIC-confirm — confirm SLA tiers with Ops]

**Q: What counts as an emergency?**
A: Emergencies include: power trip, pipe/water burst, gas leak, fire alarm, locked out, lift fault. Please flag these immediately.

A: [GENERIC-confirm — confirm after-hours emergency contact with Ops]
